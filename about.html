About this project

Audience and need

Everyday U.S. filers who know the common deductions but may not know about less obvious credits and deductions. The tool helps them know what to look up. It never calculates a tax bill.

Methodology

A curated list of 13 provisions, each with a written rule. A rule looks at your answers (filing status, income band, income sources, family, education, savings and health) and returns a strong match, a “worth checking” match, or nothing. Rules are plain code, not AI guesses, so results are repeatable and testable. Income is collected only as a range because eligibility screens rarely need exact figures.

Privacy

Answers are held in your browser’s session storage and cleared when you close the tab or press “Clear my answers.” No data is sent anywhere, and no names, SSNs or addresses are requested.

Limitations

Income bands are coarse, and phase-out limits vary by filing status and year, so every result needs confirmation. Dollar thresholds are intentionally omitted because they change yearly. The list is not exhaustive. Entries show “not yet verified” until checked against official sources.

Sources and AI disclosure

Links go to IRS.gov and the US Code. AI (Claude) helped draft the code and plain-English summaries; the student verified and edited them. See README for the full AI Use Note.
